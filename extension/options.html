SynoChan extension

Connect the extension to SynoChan on your Synology NAS. Both values are in SynoChan: ⋯ > Settings > Browser extension.

Using it

  • Right click a thread or board link on 4chan and choose Add link to SynoChan.
  • On a thread or board page, right click the page and choose Add this page to SynoChan, or press Ctrl+Shift+Y. If the shortcut does nothing, another extension may use it: set one at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  • The toolbar button adds the current page or a list of links, and opens SynoChan in DSM.
  • When the NAS cannot be reached (e.g. without VPN), links are kept and sent once it can.

With a self-signed certificate, open the server address in a tab once and accept the certificate, or the extension cannot connect.
